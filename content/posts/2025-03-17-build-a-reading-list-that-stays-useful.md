A reading list can quickly become a collection of titles you feel guilty about not finishing. To make it more useful, give each item a reason for being there and a simple next action.

For each book or article, record:

- the title and author;
- why it caught your attention;
- whether you have started it;
- one note about what you want to learn from it.

You do not need a complicated rating system. The most useful question may be: “Would I still choose this today?” If the answer is no, remove it. A shorter list that helps you decide is better than a complete archive you never open.

Try keeping separate groups for **Next**, **In progress**, and **Finished**. This makes the next choice visible and keeps completed items from competing with things you still want to read.
