It is easy to stop working at the end of a day with a project still open and no clear idea where to begin tomorrow. The task has not changed, but the context has faded.

Before stopping, leave yourself a restart note. Keep it concrete:

> Next: run the build, compare the generated navigation with the config, then update the missing section.

That is more helpful than “continue the site.” It names an action, a check, and a likely outcome.

## A useful stopping point

You do not need to finish a whole feature before taking a break. Stop after recording:

- what is already working;
- what is still unclear;
- the first action you will take when you return.

This small habit is useful for code, writing, study, and household projects. It lowers the cost of starting again, which often matters more than making a perfect plan.
