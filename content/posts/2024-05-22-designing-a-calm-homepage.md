A personal homepage has a small job: help a visitor understand whose site this is and where to go next. It does not need to explain every project or show every link at once.

I find it useful to prioritize three things:

1. **Identity:** a name and a short description.
2. **Orientation:** a small number of clear navigation choices.
3. **Recent work:** a few posts or projects that show what the site contains.

Whitespace is part of the design. It gives each section room to be understood and makes the important links easier to find. A page can feel finished without filling every empty area.

## Design for the next edit

The homepage will change as the site grows. Keep repeated details in configuration where possible, use consistent content structure, and avoid adding a component that will be difficult to update later.

A calm design is not only about appearance. It also makes the site easier to read and easier to maintain.
